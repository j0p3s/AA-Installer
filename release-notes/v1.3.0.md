## AA Installer 1.3.0

**Safer updates and crash reports.**

### New
- **Update check before installing.** If the installed app is signed with a different key (for example a modded app over the Play Store version, or a car-enabled APK over the original) or is newer, AA Installer explains why Android can't update it and offers **Uninstall & install**. It warns first that the app's data will be lost.
- **Uninstall & retry** button when an install fails for those reasons.
- Detects **car copies signed with an older key** (after reinstalling AA Installer or clearing its data) and offers to replace them.
- **Crash reporter**: if AA Installer ever crashes, the next launch shows the report with *Copy & close*.

### Improved
- Large apps (180 MB+) can be car-patched (`largeHeap`).
- Clearer signature and downgrade error messages.

### Install
Download `AA-Installer-v1.3.0.apk` below. It installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
Needs **Shizuku** (no root) or **root**. See the [User guide](https://github.com/j0p3s/AA-Installer/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).
