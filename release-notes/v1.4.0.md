## AA Installer 1.4.0

**Save & share car apps, single-APK merging, parked-only car patch.**

### New
- **Save APK / Share** after building a car-patched app: keep it anywhere or send it to another phone.
- **Save APK only** (Car copy) and **Car-enable and save APK only** (Install): build without installing, and without Shizuku or root.
- **Single APK** (on by default): split APKs (APKS/XAPK/APKM) are merged into one standalone APK.

### Changed
- **Car apps are now parked-only.** The patch follows [Android Auto Maker](https://github.com/malebuffy/AndroidAutoMaker)'s parked-game mode: the app is declared a game, listed in Android Auto's launcher, and every "distraction-optimised" claim is removed. Android Auto shows it while the car is stopped.

### Also in this line
- Crash fix for the Car copy tab on RedMagic/nubia Android 16 (1.3.1)
- **Open Android Auto** opens Android Auto's own settings (1.3.2)
- Update check with **Uninstall & install** when signatures differ (1.3.0)

### Install
Download `AA-Installer-v1.4.0.apk` below. It installs over any earlier version and keeps your data. Installing still needs **Shizuku** or **root**; saving APKs doesn't. See the [User guide](https://github.com/j0p3s/AA-Installer/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).
