## AA Installer 1.1.0

**CarBridge car patch.**

- **Car copy**: builds "*App* (Car)", a renamed `<package>.car` copy of any installed app that Android Auto lists and shows full screen (meant for use while parked). The original is untouched.
- **Car-enable before installing**: patches a picked APK or bundle and keeps the package name.
- Options: *Fill the car screen*, *Keep media services*.
- Patched APKs are signed on the device (Android Keystore) and verified before installing.
- The project is now **GPL-3.0**. The car patch is ported from [CarBridge](https://github.com/jmwerk/CarBridge) by jmwerk.
